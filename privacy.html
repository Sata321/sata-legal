Privacy Policy

How Sata handles information on your device.

Sata is a personal bonsai care journal for iPhone and iPad. This Privacy Policy explains what the app stores, which system permissions it may ask for, and what it deliberately does not do. Please read it together with the Terms of Use. By using Sata you acknowledge this policy.

In short: Sata has no account system, no backend of its own for garden data, and no cloud sync of your trees or photos. The care records you create stay inside the app’s private storage on your device unless you choose to export something yourself (for example by saving a care page image to Photos).

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Sata mobile application distributed through the Apple App Store. It describes practices for the software running on your device. It does not replace Apple’s privacy terms for the App Store, device operating system, payment processing, or optional Apple services such as speech recognition when those run on Apple’s systems.

What Sata stores on your device

When you use Sata, the app may keep the following kinds of information locally so the journal works offline:

  • Tree profiles you create or edit, including names, species, placement notes, acquired dates, watering intervals, and free-form notes about feeding, pruning, wiring, or other care.
  • Photo copies you take with the camera or import from your library for a tree’s photo log and growth comparisons. Sata stores its own copies inside the app; it does not alter or delete the originals in your photo library.
  • Care log entries (for example Water, Feed, Prune) and the reminder schedules you configure so the Care tab can show what is due.
  • Care page sets you generate, saved as image files inside the app so you can revisit or export them later.
  • Your Tree Credit balance and a local ledger of purchase and spend events, used so each verified purchase is applied only once and so creating a page set can spend one credit reliably.
  • Simple preferences such as appearance (system, light, or dark) and whether first-visit prompts have already been shown.

This information is written to the app’s sandboxed storage on the device. Sata does not operate a garden database in the cloud and does not upload your trees, photos, notes, or credit ledger to a Sata server.

Permissions and when they are requested

Sata asks for system permissions only when they are needed for a feature you start, except for the optional notification prompt described below.

  • Camera is requested when you tap Take Photo, so you can photograph a bonsai for the journal.
  • Photos (read) is requested when you tap Choose Photo, so you can pick an existing picture of a tree.
  • Photos (add only) is requested when you tap Save Image or Save All, so finished care pages can be written to your library if you choose.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition are requested when you tap Speak Notes next to a text field, so spoken words can become text. Recognition uses on-device processing when the device supports it; otherwise Apple’s speech service may process the audio under Apple’s own privacy terms. Sata does not keep audio recordings of dictation.
  • Notifications may be requested once on your first visit to the home garden screen. Sata does not schedule or send push or local notifications for care reminders. Reminders are shown inside the app in the Care tab. The prompt exists so the system permission sheet can appear; declining does not remove in-app reminders.

Sata does not request App Tracking Transparency and does not use the advertising identifier to track you across other companies’ apps or websites. You can change camera, photos, microphone, speech, and notification permissions anytime in the iOS Settings app. Turning a permission off only disables the matching action; the rest of the journal continues to work.

Purchases and payment data

Tree Credits are sold as consumable in-app purchases through Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes payment and billing. Sata never sees your full payment card details, Apple ID password, or Apple billing address. After Apple confirms a transaction, Sata stores the transaction identifier and credit amount on the device so the purchase is credited exactly once. Refunds are handled by Apple; when Apple reports a refund, Sata removes only unused credits associated with that purchase. Care pages already created remain on the device.

Network use

Day-to-day journaling does not require an account or a Sata cloud. The app may use the network to:

  • Complete App Store purchase verification with Apple.
  • Load the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and Support pages in an in-app browser when you open those links.

Those legal and support pages are static documents hosted for display; opening them does not send your garden data to the hosting site as part of the journal feature.

What Sata does not do

  • No sign-up, login account, profile, or social graph inside Sata.
  • No analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking products built into the app for measuring you across other apps.
  • No sale or rental of your garden content to third parties.
  • No collection of contacts, precise location for marketing, health records, or financial account numbers beyond what Apple already handles for App Store payments.
  • No requirement to share personal identity documents to use the journal.

Log Out and deleting data

Log Out returns you to the welcome screens without wiping your garden. Clear All Data removes trees, photo copies stored by Sata, care pages, reminders, and remaining Tree Credits from the app on this device. Uninstalling the app also removes the app’s local data. Because garden content is not mirrored to a Sata cloud, deleted local data cannot be recovered from Sata after it is cleared.

Children

Sata is a general-audience gardening journal. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children for an online profile. Parents who allow a child to use the device should supervise camera, photos, and purchase settings through standard iOS parental controls.

International users

Sata stores garden data on the device where the app is installed. If you travel or move devices, that data moves with the device (or is lost if the device is erased) unless you personally export images or notes. There is no Sata-operated cross-border transfer of your tree journal.

Third parties

Apple provides the App Store, StoreKit, optional speech recognition, and system permission dialogs. Their handling of payment and system data is governed by Apple’s policies. Hosting for the legal HTML pages is separate static hosting used only to display those documents in the in-app browser.

Changes to this policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised text will replace the previous version on this page. Continued use of Sata after an update means you accept the revised policy for future use of the app.

Contact

For privacy questions about Sata, use the Support link on the App Store product page and describe your question without attaching private photos or unnecessary personal details.
