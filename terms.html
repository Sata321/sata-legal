What Sata is

Sata is a personal bonsai care journal. It is designed to help you:

  • Keep records of individual trees, including names, species, placement, and acquired dates.
  • Log care actions such as watering, feeding, pruning, and wiring.
  • Store growth photos and compare them over time.
  • See due and overdue care items in the Care tab based on intervals and dates you enter.
  • Create care page image sets for species in your collection, using Tree Credits when you choose to generate a finished set.

Sata calculates reminder timing only from the dates and intervals you provide. It does not diagnose pests or diseases, control irrigation hardware, appraise tree value, or replace advice from a qualified horticulturist.
