Help for common questions about the Sata bonsai care journal.

How do I add a tree?

Tap the plus button in the tab bar. Optionally add a photo with Take Photo or Choose Photo. Enter the tree name, choose a species, set the acquired date and watering interval, then tap Save Tree. You can dictate into a field with Speak Notes after allowing microphone and speech access.

How are care dates calculated?

Watering is due a set number of days after the last Water log, or after the acquired date if nothing is logged yet. Reminders for feeding, pruning, wiring, and custom actions repeat on the interval you set. Due and overdue items appear in the Care tab. Tapping Done logs the action for today. Sata does not send push notifications for these reminders.

What are Care Clips?

Care Clips are short bundled videos about basic bonsai care topics. Tap a video to play or pause. Use the expand control for a full-screen player. Clips are stored in the app bundle and do not stream from the internet.

Why do care pages use a credit?

Each finished care page set is rendered as PNG images, typically one page per species in the set. Previewing is free. Creating the finished set spends one Tree Credit. Canceling or failing before save does not spend a credit. Saving images to Photos later is free.

Where is my data stored?

Only inside Sata on this device. There is no Sata account and no cloud copy of your garden. Uninstalling the app or using Clear All Data removes trees, photo copies kept by Sata, care pages, reminders, and remaining credits.

What does Log Out do?

Log Out returns you to the welcome screens. It does not erase your garden. Use Clear All Data if you want to delete everything on this device.

I turned off camera, photos, or microphone

Open the iOS Settings app, find Sata, and turn the permission back on. You can still type instead of dictating, or choose a library photo instead of using the camera, depending on what you allow.

A purchase did not appear

Keep Sata open briefly while online. Verified purchases that were interrupted are applied when the app processes finished App Store transactions. There is no restore button for Tree Credits because they are consumable and stored on this device. For refunds or billing questions, contact Apple.

Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

Open them from the welcome screen footer or from Settings → About. They load inside Sata in a built-in browser over HTTPS.

Still need help?

Use the Support link on the Sata App Store page and describe what happened. Please do not include personal photos or private details you do not want shared in email.
